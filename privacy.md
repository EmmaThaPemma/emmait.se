# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 29, 2026_

## What is Collected

When you visit my site, my hosting provider **Cloudflare** automatically logs:

- Your IP address
- Browser type and device info
- Pages you visit and when
- Basic technical request data (status codes, cache hits/misses)

This data is mainly processed by Cloudflare

## How *I* Use It

I don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. I don't use cookies to track you. I do use it to:

- To keep the site running, fast, and secure
- To detect abuse and security threats
- To respond to you if you reach out

If you contact me or fill out a form, I collect whatever you choose to send me (name, email, message, etc.).

## How Cloudflare *claims* to use it

Cloudflare acts as my [data processor][1]. See:

- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- [Cloudflare DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/)
- [Cloudflare GDPR FAQ](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/gdpr/)

Cloudflare claims to retains this log data for a limited period and claims not to use it to build profiles of individual users for advertising.

## Data Retention

Cloudflare claims to keep edge log data for a few days, then deletes it. Anything you submit to me directly is kept as long as needed.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live (EU, UK, California, etc.), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete Your personal data. To do so, contact me at **[kontakt@emmait.se][2]**.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email me at **[kontakt@emmait.se][2]**.
