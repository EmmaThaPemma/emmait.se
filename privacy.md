# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 29, 2026_

## What We Collect

When you visit our site, our hosting provider **Cloudflare** automatically logs:

- Your IP address
- Browser type and device info
- Pages you visit and when
- Basic technical request data (status codes, cache hits/misses)

We don't use cookies to track you. If we enable [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), it's cookie-free and doesn't track individuals across sites.

If you contact us or fill out a form, we collect whatever you choose to send us (name, email, message, etc.).

## How We Use It

- To keep the site running, fast, and secure
- To detect abuse and security threats
- To respond to you if you reach out

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

## Cloudflare's Role

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare acts as our data processor. See:

- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- [Cloudflare DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/)
- [Cloudflare GDPR FAQ](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/gdpr/)

## Data Retention

Cloudflare keeps edge log data for a few days, then deletes it. Anything you submit to us directly is kept only as long as needed.
